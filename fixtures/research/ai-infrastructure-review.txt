# AI infrastructure review

This reviewed fixture defines a bounded AI-infrastructure theme for v0.4.
Compute demand depends on advanced semiconductor manufacturing and packaging,
high-bandwidth memory, networking, datacenters and cooling, and generation,
grid, and electrical equipment. The linked company relationships are reviewed
interpretations for research use and require source-backed revision before a
production investment decision.

The theme is weakened if demand growth becomes inventory-led rather than
deployment-led, if supply substitution removes the bottleneck, or if power
interconnection delays prevent datacenter capacity from being commissioned.
