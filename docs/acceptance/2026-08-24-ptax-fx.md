## Decision

The project now collects the official BCB PTAX closing USD/BRL bulletin separately
from BCB SGS macro series, retains the exact OData response before parsing, publishes
manifest-backed canonical FX Parquet, resolves one requested fixing date at an
explicit decision time, and converts with policy
`ptax_sale_direct_inverse_1_0_0`.

The admitted rate is BRL per USD and preserves both buy and sell values. Conversion
always uses the sell side: USD to BRL multiplies and BRL to USD divides. The policy
does not carry a previous fixing across a missing date, construct a midpoint, switch
rate sides, guess inversion, or triangulate another pair. Every non-identity result
includes the exact observation and policy pin.

This is a bounded USD/BRL mechanism, not a broad currency catalog. Additional pairs
remain unsupported until they receive their own source and policy admission.

fixing, publication, and availability instant. Local durable receipt remains a

FX input.
