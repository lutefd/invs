## Decision

The bounded US and Brazil slices pass a retained, executable point-in-time bias
audit. The audit verifies exact one-microsecond before/at/after boundaries, hashes
all referenced source and canonical evidence, rejects incomplete regional category
coverage, rejects current-universe substitution, rejects receipt-time datasets
relabelled as historically safe, and requires unsupported corporate actions to
block rather than produce a plausible adjusted result.

This closes v0.2. It does not create a strategy, backtester, portfolio, optimizer,
or simulated brokerage. It also does not turn the bounded evidence into broad US or
Brazil coverage. Research outside the admitted securities, dates, calendars,
currencies, and action policies must still fail closed.

## Fitness decisions

The accepted audit labels every dataset it can reach:

- `backtest_safe`: bounded Nasdaq/B3 identity and membership notices, exact-artifact
  XNAS/BVMF calendars, ALFRED CPI vintages, the exact SEC split, SEC filing metadata,
  and the admitted PTAX USD/BRL fixing.
- `installation_replay_only`: retained Yahoo AAPL chart prices, official PETZ3
  COTAHIST rows, and the B3 corporate-action sample family.
- `current_research_only`: current FRED/BCB convenience pulls and other current
  snapshots remain outside this audit and must not be substituted for admitted
  historical inputs.
- `unsupported`: the latest B3 sample action state blocks the affected interval;
  absent broad calendar, lifecycle, pair, or action coverage also fails closed.

Yahoo `.SA` remains unadmitted for the Brazil bridge. CVM CAD remains raw-only. A
missing source, unavailable historic timestamp, unsupported action, or out-of-range
calendar is a visible scope decision, never permission to use the current universe
or newest revision.

## Executable controls

`invs-bias-audit` and the Make targets validate a strict specification and publish
an immutable result. The test suite deliberately mutates:

- a missing required regional category;
- an installation-replay dataset relabelled as safe;
- a current-only membership substituted for historical membership;
- a filing leaked one microsecond early;
- a non-exact decision clock;
- an unsupported action claimed eligible;
- a modified evidence file; and
- a modified audit manifest.

The validator also requires exactly one probe for every admitted regional category,
canonical UTC clocks, complete dataset usage, nonempty scope decisions, and matching
SHA-256 for every evidence path.
